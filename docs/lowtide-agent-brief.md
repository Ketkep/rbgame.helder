# Brief for agents building a LOWTIDE chapter (read this, then `docs/lowtide.md`, then the code it points to)

You build ONE chapter of Lowtide, a two-player online horror campaign (the game is "Trust Me…"; this campaign reuses the whole 2 Player Mode co-op stack).
Read first: `docs/lowtide.md` (story, roles, endings), `docs/campaign3.md` (co-op API: `w.coop`: set/get/on, emit/onEvent, zone, presence, tell, rng, death rules),
`src/levels/lowtide/intake.js` (the finished-look reference level), `src/levels/lowtide/kit.js`, `horror.js`, `stalker.js`, `scare.js`, `roles.js`, `sound.js`.

## Hard requirements
* **>= 10 minutes for a first-time pair, aim for 12-15.** Long, multi-stage (7+ beats), no padding walking: every beat is a puzzle, a scare set piece, a chase, a reveal, or a choice. The bots' sim time is NOT human time (humans ≈ 3x + talking + dying + hiding).
* Genuinely two-player: every beat needs both of you (different views, different info, one holds the torch/the lever, one distracts the Orderly, etc.). Use `roles.js`: the run has a secret Keyholder and Subject; from chapter 3 on use private files/whispers via `R.whisper()` / `note()` with per-role text. Never reveal the roles before chapter 7 (unless your chapter plan says so).
* **Looks matter most.** Dark, grimy, beautiful. Use the kit (`room`, `door`, `bed`, `locker`, `curtain`, `gurney`, `desk`, `decal`, `stencil`, `pipes`, `barWindow`, `H.lamp` with modes, the torch). Do not use the bright 2 Player Mode kit pieces (`deck`, `lantern`, `sign`, `plate` with glowing neon) except re-skinned. Every room needs: lamps (mix of steady/buzz/dying/dead), 2+ decals, 2+ props, a clear silhouette you can read in torchlight. Take real-render screenshots with `BASE=http://localhost:PORT/ node tools/lt-shot.mjs <chapter> '[[x,y,z,yawDeg,pitchDeg,"name"],...]'` (images land in /tmp/shots/LT<ch>-<name>.png; view them with Read) and fix anything flat, overlit, clipping, or unreadable. Then re-shoot.
* Scares: dread first, then payoff. Use `scare.js` (`Z.rush/behind/stand/partnerWrong/blackout/sting/whisper/knock`) through `Z.fire(id, {role})` so both machines agree on timing. A chapter has at most ~6 big stings (`Z.maxBig`); a jump scare must be earned by 60+ seconds of tension and never repeat the same trick twice in a row. The Orderly (`stalker.js`) is not killable; he must always be learnable: his wheels squeak, steps carry, torch flickers near him, a heartbeat starts. Provide hiding spots (`st.hideSpot`) and a way to lure him (`st.noise`).
* Fair deaths: any death is the Orderly (or a scripted fall); checkpoints (`kit.checkpoint`) at every part, away from the Orderly's patrol path, and a respawn must never put you in his sight.
* Narration: the voice is the **Night Desk**, a tired, kind hospital intercom that slowly slips (contradicts itself, uses a name you never gave, whispers to one of you). Calm, short, procedural, unsettling; no jokes. Add lines to `src/script.js` as ONE block at the end (`'lt.cN.<name>': ['line', 'alt']`, N = your chapter number), use `c.tell('p1'|'p2'|'all', key)`. Different lines per role when it matters.
* No external assets: all procedural (WebAudio, canvas, three.js). Do not touch other chapters, the 2 Player Mode levels or `kit.js` of `levels/coop` (tiny backward-compatible additions to `src/levels/lowtide/*` are fine and welcome: new props, new decal kinds, new scare helpers; keep them general and mention them in your report).
* Bots: `window.__noRender` is true in the bot harness; the stalker never catches bots (`st.bot`), `door()` auto-opens unlocked doors for bots, but puzzles (levers, notes, items, codes) need explicit `botSteps(w, {p1:[…], p2:[…]})` (see `src/levels/coop/kit.js`, steps: x,z,r,until,jump,use,follow,face,wait…). `EXTRA="&camp=lowtide" BASE=http://localhost:PORT/ node tools/coop-bot.mjs <chapter> 1200` must COMPLETE three times in a row, and `BASE=… node tools/test-coop.mjs` must pass.
* The two machines simulate their own player and the whole level locally: anything a player does that changes physics/state must go through `w.coop` (set/emit/zone/presence). Time-driven things (functions of `w.t`) are fine. Interactables must NOT sit inside a solid collider (focus ray is blocked): put handles in front of walls.

## Where things go
* Level file: `src/levels/lowtide/<name>.js` (+ optional `<name>-kit.js`), `export default { id: 'lowtideN', name, music: 'l1', horror: true, deathRule: 'self', completeQuip, titleCam, build(w, game) }`.
* Register: in `src/levels/lowtide/roster.js` add the import and put it in `BUILT` at index N-1 (keep `intake` etc. as they are).
* Spawn near z=4 like every co-op level, level runs toward -z. Title: chapters have NO neon sign; show the chapter name with `stencil()` on a wall near the start ("CHAPTER N · NAME").
* Story flags between chapters: `R.flag(key, value)` / `R.get(key)`. Reserved names: `took-key` (ch3), `heard-tape` (ch5), `trusted` (ch6: whether each player picked the real partner), `locked-in` (ch7: the Keyholder locked the Subject in), `forgave` (ch8). Add your own with a `ch<N>-` prefix.

## Chapter plans (story detail in docs/lowtide.md)
1 Intake: extend the existing slice (`intake.js`) into a 10+ min chapter: wake in restraints (one is freed by the other: hold-to-undo straps), wristband with your partner's name, first torch, first locked doors, first Orderly patrol seen through a window, tutorial of hiding and luring, a lights-out corridor, a sealed door that needs both of you at two far consoles, the first "Night Desk" slip. Ends at the lift to Ward B.
2 Night Rounds: Ward B patrol puzzle. One lures with noise, one hacks the nurses' station; lockers, vents, a long chase down an endless corridor loop, a 2-minute hide-and-seek in the dormitory with a heartbeat meter.
3 Records Room: asymmetric files (each of you reads a different version of the same patient file), the key hunt, first private note ("take the key" for the Keyholder), first "partner is wrong" glitches (`Z.partnerWrong`), a dark archive maze with sliding shelves.
4 Hydrotherapy: flooded wing with rising water on a timer (visual water plane + a lethal-but-telegraphed depth), air pockets/ledges, valves needing both, the Orderly in the water (he cannot swim fast), a drowned corridor lit only by torches.
5 The Theatre: one of you strapped on the table, the other operating the machine (roles swap mid-chapter), gore set pieces (imagery only), a truth fragment (photo of you both in uniforms) and a tape you can choose to play (`heard-tape`).
6 The Morgue: torch-only darkness; the doppelganger partner (two avatars; prove which is real with something only your real partner can do: a shared handshake action, a code agreed earlier), voices from behind, drawers that open when you are not looking.
7 The Director: roles are revealed; the betrayal chapter. The Keyholder may lock the Subject in (private choice with a visible timer); the Subject can sabotage the lock; flags `locked-in` etc.
8 Lowtide: the tide comes in; the finale, every mechanic remixed, a long chase, and the final choice that selects one of three endings (together / alone / the tide takes the ward).
